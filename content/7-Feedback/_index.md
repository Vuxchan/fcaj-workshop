Below are my personal reflections and feedback following my participation in the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program:

### 1. Overall Evaluation

* **Working Environment:** The environment is highly dynamic, welcoming, and open. Team members are always ready to offer support, share domain knowledge, and foster an inspiring atmosphere for interns to learn and innovate.
* **Support from Mentor / Admin Team:** Mentors provide dedicated guidance, closely monitor task progress, and foster problem-solving mindsets for real-world scenarios. The admin team ensures seamless access to resources, documentation, and flexible scheduling.
* **Relevance to Academic Major:** The internship content focuses heavily on AWS Cloud computing, Data Engineering, and Artificial Intelligence (AI), which aligns directly with my university major and current technological trends.
* **Learning & Growth Opportunities:** The program provides hands-on exposure to practical AWS services, specialized workshops, and improves both technical competencies (Cloud/AI) and professional soft skills (teamwork, communication, report writing).
* **Culture & Team Spirit:** The strong "Pay it forward" culture and team cohesion enabled me to gain practical experience and expand my professional network.

### 2. Specific Feedback Questions

* **Most Satisfying Aspect:** Being able to work directly with AWS infrastructure, participating in major workshops, and receiving clear career roadmap advice from industry speakers and mentors.
* **Areas for Improvement:** Expanding some study sessions into interactive project-sharing and knowledge-exchange sessions, allowing members to learn from each other's work.
* **Recommendation to Peers:** I will definitely recommend this program to my peers, as it bridges the gap between academic theory and actual industry demands.

### 3. Suggestions & Future Goals

* **Suggestions:** Hosting mini-hackathons or short group challenges where interns can collaborate to solve practical Cloud/AI problems.
* **Future Intentions:** I hope to continue contributing to and learning with the FCAJ community in future activities, staying updated on Cloud Infrastructure & Generative AI advancements.

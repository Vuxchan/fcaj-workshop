# EVENT REPORT: "AWS: Enterprise Cloud Architectures and Industry Application"

* **Event Name:** AWS: Enterprise Cloud Architectures and Industry Application featuring Cloud Kinetics & Renova Cloud
* **Date & Time:** July 4th, 2026
* **Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 02 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City
* **Role:** Attendee

---

### 1. Event Objectives
Organized as a study tour in collaboration between Swinburne Vietnam and the *AWS First Cloud AI Journey* community to help students:
- Explore cloud computing trends, infrastructure architectures, and enterprise applications directly from industry experts.
- Stay updated on market trends, recruitment demands, and career development roadmaps in the AI era.
- Bridge the gap between academic theory and real-world enterprise requirements.

### 2. Speakers & Connectors
- **Nguyen Gia Hung:** Head of Solution Architect at AWS Vietnam, Founder of *AWS First Cloud AI Journey* community.
- **Banh Cam Vinh:** Data Engineer at Renova Cloud, AWS Community Builder (Data specialization).
- **Nhu Tran:** Account Manager at AWS Vietnam (Startup segment).
- **Khang Nguyen:** Solution Architect at Cloud Kinetics, Alumnus (Batch 1) of Swinburne Vietnam.
- **Connector:** Nguyen Tran Minh Duy, Corporate Relations Officer at Swinburne Vietnam.

### 3. Key Highlights & Main Activities
- **Cloud Market Landscape:** Insights into cloud growth in Vietnam, key industry sectors (finance, retail, manufacturing), and AWS investment strategies in local infrastructure & talent.
- **Deep Data Engineering:** Understanding differences between clean textbook data and production data challenges; introduction to the Data Foundation DNA framework (Ingestion, Processing, Storage, etc.).
- **Soft Skills & Mindset:** Overcoming fear of failure, mastering small talk & communication with leadership, and building community visibility.
- **AI Mindset:** Using AI as a capability amplifier rather than a replacement for human reasoning, emphasizing core fundamentals over blind reliance on AI tools.

### 4. Key Value Gained
- **Core Fundamentals Matter:** Computer networking, database management, and data structures/algorithms serve as essential foundations for all IT roles.
- **Growth Mindset:** Emphasizing lifelong learning and resilience in facing technical and career challenges.
- **Business Problem Understanding:** Recognizing that solving real business problems is even more crucial than writing raw code.
- **Responsibility & Integrity:** Striving to exceed client/manager expectations and taking full ownership of built solutions.

### 5. Application to Work & Career Development
- **Building Side Projects:** Developing industry-specific projects to stand out against AI-driven CV screeners.
- **Using Standard Frameworks:** Applying standard architectural guidance like the *AWS Well-Architected Framework* for scalable and secure designs.
- **Enhancing Workflows with AI:** Leveraging AI tools (such as Amazon Q) for code scanning, productivity optimization, and architectural reasoning.
- **Networking & Community Engagement:** Actively engaging in tech communities, asking insightful questions, and building professional relationships.

### 6. Event Experience & Reflections
- **Inspiring Student Dedication:** Impressed by students traveling 180km from Can Tho starting at 5:00 AM to attend.
- **Open Interactive Sessions:** Direct Q&A opportunities with speakers and live small talk demo exercises.
- **Community Spirit:** Honest career failure stories shared by speakers, demonstrating resilience and the "Pay it forward" culture of the AWS community.
